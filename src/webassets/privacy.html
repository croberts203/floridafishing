Fish Bite Forecast Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Applies to Fish Bite Forecast at fishforecast.net, including when it's installed to your home screen.

Fish Bite Forecast is a free hobby app run by Chris Roberts (Tie & Tide) in Jacksonville, Florida. This page explains what the app stores, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We do not sell your data or show ads.

You can use the forecast without an account. Without one, your spots, catch log and settings stay in your browser on that device. If you sign in, your account is shared with our Fly Bench app (app.flytyingrecipes.org) — one login works in both.

When you pick a tackle shop and tap Call, Website or Directions, we record which shop and which button (and your account, if signed in) so we can tell shops how many people they hear from. Calls, maps and websites open in your phone's own apps.

Your location is used only when you tap 📍 or add a spot, and the camera only when you take a photo.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, photo storage and our helper functions (United States).
  • NOAA, the National Weather Service and USGS supply tides, marine forecasts, buoys, river gauges and maps. Open-Meteo supplies weather. To get the forecast for a spot, its map position is sent to these services — never anything about you.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies boat ramps when you turn that map layer on; the area you're viewing is sent.
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes photos you choose to send to identify a fish or read the water. Photos are sent only when you tap the button, used only for that request, and not used to train AI models under Anthropic's commercial terms.
  • Your phone's notification service (Apple or Google) delivers bite alerts if you turn them on.
  • GitHub Pages serves the app's files. Like any web server it briefly logs IP addresses for security.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your login and everything stored with it in both Fish Bite Forecast and Fly Bench: spots, catch log and photos, alerts, crew memberships and posts, recipes and materials. Backup copies kept by our hosting provider expire within a few days.

Data kept only on your device (if you never signed in) can be cleared by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

If you can't open the app, request deletion through the contact form using the email address on your account, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Children

Fish Bite Forecast is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each member's private data is protected by database rules so only that member's signed-in account can read it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Questions: use the contact form.
